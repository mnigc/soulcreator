A macro investment research platform covering gold decisions, macro regime, global liquidity, and a weighted portfolio signal board. Data syncs automatically every trading day, turning scattered macro data into an actionable view.

## Features

- **Portfolio signal board** — Gold, liquidity, macro regime, and equity-cycle signals are weighted into a single composite score, so one page shows the market's overall lean.
- **Gold decision** — Gold/USD correlation, two-factor pricing residuals, central-bank gold buying, and momentum indicators cross-check each other to answer whether gold is expensive and still worth buying.
- **Macro regime** — Based on S&P500 and macro-regime backtests it identifies the current regime and flags risk anomalies, plus equity risk premium and style-rotation references.
- **Global liquidity** — Balance sheets and net liquidity of the Fed, ECB, and BoJ, layered with SOFR funding rates, to track the global liquidity cycle.
- **Thematic analysis** — Yield curve, inflation expectations, credit stress, cross-asset correlation, macro consensus, commodities, and leading indicators — six standalone pages you can dig into individually.
- **Automatic data sync** — Standalone Python scripts pull from yfinance, FRED, and gold-api; GitHub Actions writes into Supabase automatically every trading day.
