Automatically detect price numbers on any webpage and convert them to your local currency in one click. No copy-paste, no new tabs — exchange rates sync in real time across 150+ currency pairs.

## Features

- **Smart price detection** — A deep-learning model automatically scans pages for price patterns, covering common global formats like ¥1,299, $19.99, and €12.50 — no manual selection needed.
- **One-click conversion** — Detection results show right on the page; click to switch the target currency. Zero interruption to your browsing.
- **Real-time rates** — Connected to aggregated global financial-market APIs, rates refresh automatically every morning with under 2-hour latency.
- **150+ currencies** — Covers major currencies like USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, CNY, HKD, and KRW, plus niche currencies from Southeast Asia, Africa, and the Middle East.
- **Multilingual UI** — The extension UI supports multiple languages including Chinese, English, Japanese, and Korean.
- **Privacy safe** — All computation happens locally in your browser. No browsing history or page content is ever uploaded.
